Short Bio

Jingfeng Wu is an Assistant Professor of Statistics at UC Berkeley. His research focuses on deep learning theory, optimization, and statistical learning. He was a postdoctoral fellow at the Simons Institute at UC Berkeley. He earned his PhD from Johns Hopkins University, and MS and BS from Peking University.

  • Assistant Professor (2026-now) of Statistics at UC Berkeley.
  • Postdoc (2023-2026) at Simons Institute at UC Berkeley, hosted by Peter Bartlett and Bin Yu.
  • PhD (2019-2023) in Computer Science at JHU, advised by Vova Braverman.
  • BS & MS (2012-2016-2019) in Mathematics & Applied Math at Peking University.
